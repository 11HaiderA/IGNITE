Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 12, 2026

This Privacy Policy applies to the mobile game application Match Sticks (Application ID: com.matchsticks.puzzle). We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting it through our compliance with this policy.

1. Information We Collect

Match Sticks does not require user registration or account creation. We do not collect or store personal details such as your name, email address, physical address, or phone number.

Game progress (such as solved levels, stars, and audio settings) is stored locally on your device using device storage and is not uploaded to any private server.

2. Third-Party Services & Advertising

The application integrates third-party services that may automatically collect information used to identify your device. These third-party services include:

  • Google Play Services: Used for core application infrastructure and platform services. (Google Privacy Policy)
  • Google AdMob: Used to display interstitial and rewarded advertisements. AdMob may collect and process device identifiers (including the Google Advertising ID), IP addresses, and diagnostic/performance data to serve relevant ads, prevent fraud, and measure ad engagement. (How Google uses information from sites or apps)

3. Advertising ID & Opt-Out

You can reset or opt out of personalized advertising at any time on your Android device by navigating to:

Settings > Google > Ads > Delete advertising ID or Reset advertising ID.

4. Children’s Privacy (COPPA)

Our game is intended for general audiences aged 13 and above. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13. If you believe a child under 13 has provided personal data through a third-party service, please contact us so we can take appropriate measures.

5. Security

We value your trust. All network transmissions with third-party service providers (such as Google AdMob) utilize industry-standard Transport Layer Security (TLS / HTTPS) encryption.
