What changed — and what deliberately didn't

✅ Kept from your screenshot

  • 4-screen flow and order: Home → Gameplay → Levels → Settings/Pause
  • Fiery orange-on-charcoal identity, match & flame motifs
  • Level 13, “Remove 2 matches” objective, 0/1-style counter (now 0/2, matching the instruction)
  • Round level nodes, star ratings, locked levels 14–20, current level 13 ringed, match-shaped toggles

✨ Improved

  • Hierarchy: one primary (gradient) button per screen; everything else is dark/secondary
  • Consistency: 8-pt spacing, 16px screen padding, 999px pill radius everywhere
  • Readability: ember texture moved to edges only; board & text sit on flat charcoal
  • States: hover lift, 4px press-down, disabled, current-level ring + lock icon (not colour alone)
  • Fix: original grid skipped 7 & 8 — this grid is sequential 1–20

Colour tokens — tap to copy

One palette for the whole game. Gradient is reserved for primary actions and the logo only.

Type, layout & spacing

Typography

  • Display: Baloo 2 ExtraBold — logo 44px, screen titles 30–36px, buttons 16–20px
  • Body / UI: Nunito ExtraBold — labels 13px, body 14–16px, line-height 1.5
  • All-caps only for buttons & titles, letter-spacing +0.04em

Layout grid

  • Phone frame: 360 × 640 dp reference, radius 36px, 3px ember stroke
  • Screen padding: 16px · Section gap: 16px · Button gap: 12px
  • Spacing scale: 4 / 8 / 12 / 16 / 24 / 32 (8-pt system)
  • Min tap target: 44 × 44px — level nodes are 42px in this compact grid (44px+ in production)

Matchstick spec

  • Stick: 56 × 8px, #F3C98A → #D9A05A, radius 4px
  • Head: 14px circle, #7A2E12 with #A63E0E highlight
  • Hit area: 24px around the stick (invisible padding)
  • Removed: 12% opacity ghost, so the player sees their progress
